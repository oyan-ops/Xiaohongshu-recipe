# 小红书食谱 - 用户指南 / User Guide

## 中文版本 / Chinese Version

### 📱 主要功能

#### 1. 提取食谱（Extract Tab）
- **功能**：从小红书链接一键提取结构化食谱
- **如何使用**：
  1. 在「提取」标签页，粘贴小红书帖子链接或分享的完整文字
  2. 系统会自动解析并生成结构化食谱
  3. 编辑完成后自动保存到选中的文件夹

#### 2. 我的食谱库（Library Tab）
- **功能**：查看、管理和组织你的所有食谱
- **如何使用**：
  - **浏览食谱**：点击食谱卡片查看完整详情（包括材料、步骤、营养信息）
  - **编辑菜谱名**：在食谱详情页标题旁的「编辑」按钮修改名称
  - **文件夹管理**：顶部按钮管理文件夹，点击不同文件夹查看相应食谱
  - **批量操作**：
    - 点「批量选择」进入批量模式
    - 逐个勾选食谱或点「全选」选择所有
    - 点「批量移动」将选中食谱移动到其他文件夹
  - **添加到购物车**：食谱卡片右下角「+」按钮加入购物车

#### 3. 做菜计划（Plan Tab）
- **功能**：规划每天做什么菜，追踪准备进度
- **如何使用**：
  1. **选择日期**：点击日历选择要规划的日期
  2. **加菜**：点「+ 加菜」按钮从食谱库选择要做的菜
  3. **标记完成**：做完后点菜谱卡片前的「☑」标记为已做
  4. **批量加购物车**：选择日期范围，点「全部加入菜单」将那段时间的菜加入购物车
  5. **共享计划**：
     - 点日期后显示「共享计划」按钮
     - 选择日期范围（如5月1-7日）或指定日期（如一三五）
     - 生成分享链接邀请他人查看

#### 4. 做菜记录（History Tab）
- **功能**：查看你曾经做过的所有菜，以及做菜频率统计
- **如何使用**：
  - **热力图**：过去12周的做菜活动一览，颜色越深表示那天用时越长
  - **常做的菜排行榜**：显示Top 5最频繁做的菜，帮助你了解自己的喜好
  - **日期详情**：点日期查看那天做过的所有菜
  - **日期范围查询**：用日历导航查看特定月份的记录

#### 5. 购物车（Cart）
- **功能**：收集要采购的所有食材
- **如何使用**：
  - **添加项目**：在食谱库或计划中点「+」加入购物车
  - **查看清单**：页面任何地方都能看到购物车数量，点击进入详情
  - **清除项目**：购物车中点「×」移除单个项目
  - **清空购物车**：点「清空」一次性清除所有项

#### 6. 食谱共享（Share Recipes）
- **功能**：邀请他人查看你的食谱库或特定文件夹
- **如何使用**：
  1. 食谱库任意位置可看到「共享」按钮
  2. 选择分享权限：「可编辑」或「只读」
  3. 设置邀请有效期（默认7天）
  4. 复制生成的链接分享给他人
  5. 接收者点击链接后可加入你的文件夹

#### 7. 计划共享（Share Meal Plan）
- **功能**：邀请他人查看你在特定日期的做菜计划
- **如何使用**：
  1. 计划标签页，点日期后出现「共享计划」按钮
  2. **日期范围模式**：输入起止日期（如5月1-5月7），自动包含该范围所有日期
  3. **指定日期模式**：逐个点选要分享的日期（如周一、三、五），支持跨月选择
  4. 设置权限和有效期后生成链接
  5. 接收者打开链接会看到你在这些日期安排的菜和权限说明

## English Version

### 📱 Main Features

#### 1. Extract Recipe (Extract Tab)
- **Function**: Extract structured recipe from Xiaohongshu (RED) links with one click
- **How to use**:
  1. Go to "Extract" tab and paste a RED post link or recipe text
  2. System automatically parses and generates a structured recipe
  3. Edit and save to your selected folder

#### 2. My Recipe Library (Library Tab)
- **Function**: View, manage and organize all your recipes
- **How to use**:
  - **Browse recipes**: Click a recipe card to see full details (ingredients, steps, nutrition info)
  - **Edit recipe name**: Click the "Edit" button next to the title in recipe detail
  - **Folder management**: Use top buttons to manage folders, click to filter recipes
  - **Batch operations**:
    - Click "Batch Select" to enter batch mode
    - Check individual recipes or click "Select All"
    - Click "Batch Move" to move selected recipes to another folder
  - **Add to cart**: Click "+" button on recipe card to add to shopping cart

#### 3. Meal Plan (Plan Tab)
- **Function**: Plan what to cook each day and track cooking progress
- **How to use**:
  1. **Select date**: Click calendar to pick a date to plan
  2. **Add recipes**: Click "+ Add Recipe" to select dishes from library
  3. **Mark complete**: Click "☑" on recipe card after cooking
  4. **Batch add to cart**: Select date range, click "Add All to Cart" to add all dishes in that period
  5. **Share plan**:
     - Click a date to reveal "Share Plan" button
     - Select date range (e.g., May 1-7) or specific dates (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri)
     - Generate and share link with others

#### 4. Cooking History (History Tab)
- **Function**: View all dishes you've cooked and cooking frequency statistics
- **How to use**:
  - **Heatmap**: 12-week activity view - darker colors mean longer cooking time
  - **Top recipes ranking**: See your Top 5 most frequently cooked dishes
  - **Date details**: Click a date to see all dishes cooked that day
  - **Browse by month**: Use calendar navigation to explore specific months

#### 5. Shopping Cart
- **Function**: Collect all ingredients you need to buy
- **How to use**:
  - **Add items**: Click "+" in library or plan to add to cart
  - **View list**: See cart count anywhere, click to view details
  - **Remove item**: Click "×" to remove single item from cart
  - **Clear cart**: Click "Clear" to remove all items at once

#### 6. Share Recipes (Share Recipes)
- **Function**: Invite others to view your recipe library or specific folders
- **How to use**:
  1. Click "Share" button in recipe library
  2. Choose permission: "Editable" or "Read-only"
  3. Set invitation expiration (default 7 days)
  4. Copy generated link and share with others
  5. Recipients click link to join your folder

#### 7. Share Meal Plan (Share Plan)
- **Function**: Invite others to view your meal plan for specific dates
- **How to use**:
  1. In Plan tab, click a date to reveal "Share Plan" button
  2. **Date range mode**: Enter start and end dates (e.g., May 1-7) to include all dates in range
  3. **Specify dates mode**: Click individual dates to select (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri), works across months
  4. Set permissions and expiration, then generate link
  5. Recipients see your planned dishes and access level

### 🎯 Tips

- **Sync with others**: Use sharing features to collaborate with family members or roommates
- **Meal planning**: Use date range sharing to sync weekly menus with household members
- **Track habits**: Check cooking history to understand your favorite dishes and cooking patterns
- **Smart shopping**: Use cart to compile all ingredients needed across multiple recipes
